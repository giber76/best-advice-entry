Entry for [The Best Advice I Ever Got](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0)
on make.fun — one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best advice
the writer ever received.

## The advice (148 characters)

> My dad, patching the roof in a storm because he had put it off all summer, said: the leak you ignore in June is the ceiling on your head in October.

## Why this sentence

- **A real moment with a cost:** dad patching the roof in a storm because
  he had put it off all summer — the advice is earned, not a slogan handed
  down from nowhere.
- **Specific, not generic:** a leak, a month, a ceiling. It could not be
  swapped into any other piece of advice.
- **True beyond the moment:** small problems ignored become the roof over
  your head — it carries past the roof into anything put off.
- **It lands:** "the ceiling on your head" is the sting the reader keeps
  after the last word, and the whole thing reads in one breath.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run locally with:

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
